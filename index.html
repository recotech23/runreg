About Innovation Fitness

A Modern Fitness Center

Founded in 2023, Innovation Fitness was established to bring a modern, well-equipped training space for athletes, daily gymgoers, and coaching clients.

Whether you want an open gym to complete your heavy lifts on dedicated Olympic platforms, prefer high-energy boxing padwork for cardio, or require doctor-guided rehabilitation after an injury, Innovation Fitness provides the space, equipment, and expertise under one roof.
